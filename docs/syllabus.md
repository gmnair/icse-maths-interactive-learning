# ICSE Class 10 Mathematics syllabus map

This learning hub is organised around the ICSE Class 10 Mathematics topics below. Notes are concise revision material, not a replacement for the Council's current syllabus, prescribed textbook, or school guidance.

| Area | Topics covered |
| --- | --- |
| Commercial Mathematics | Goods and Services Tax (GST); recurring deposits and interest |
| Algebra | Linear inequations in one variable; quadratic equations; ratio and proportion; factor and remainder theorems; matrices; arithmetic progressions; coordinate geometry |
| Geometry | Similarity; circle theorems; constructions |
| Mensuration | Surface area and volume of right circular cylinders, cones, spheres, and hemispheres; combined solids |
| Trigonometry | Trigonometric ratios and identities; heights and distances |
| Statistics | Mean, median, and mode of grouped data; histograms and cumulative frequency/ogives |
| Probability | Experimental and theoretical probability for elementary events |

Interactive tools include a GST/RD calculator, quadratic solver, JSXGraph heights-and-distances model, mensuration calculator, and Chart.js visualisations for data and probability. Use the site's worked examples and self-check prompts to practise method as well as answers.
